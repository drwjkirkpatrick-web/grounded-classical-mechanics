# Assessment Guide

Assessment here serves the CBE promise: every learner demonstrably *can do* the physics,
not merely recall it. The instruments are built into every lesson.

## The Daily Rhythm: Pre-Test → Post-Test

- **Pre-test (5 min):** 3–4 questions before teaching. Purpose is a baseline, not a grade.
  Learners who "fail" it and pass the post-test show the greatest growth — celebrate that.
- **Post-test (10 min):** 4–5 questions. Q1–2 deliberately mirror pre-test items, so
  growth per learner per lesson is measurable. Q3–5 probe the day's new idea; the final
  item stretches.
- **Recording:** a simple grid — learner × lesson, pre score, post score. Ten minutes a
  week to maintain; gold at parent meetings and for CBE learner records.

**Growth mark per lesson** = post − pre (both out of their question counts). A class
average growth of +2 or more means the lesson worked; near zero means reteach with a
different entry point, not more drilling.

## The Advanced Questions (Ages 14–17)

Each lesson carries 2–3 quantitative or multi-step questions with worked answers. Uses:

- **Differentiation:** older learners do these while younger ones consolidate the core.
- **Senior-school anchoring:** they are written at Grade 10 Physics design level and use
  the design's mathematics (P = ρgh, F = ma, W = Fd, moments, v = fλ, T = 2π√(L/g)).
- **Weekly quiz fodder:** draw Friday review quizzes from the week's advanced questions.

## Project Assessment (Rubric, 4 × 5 = 20 marks)

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Investigation design** | No plan | Plan with gaps | Variables, method and safety all explicit |
| **Data honesty** | Missing/invented data | Partial records | Raw + processed data, uncertainty admitted |
| **Iteration** | No changes | Changes without reasons | Measured improvement cycle with evidence |
| **Communication** | Cannot explain | Explains parts | Clear 3-minute demo with correct physics |

## CBE Competency Mapping

| CBE core competency | Where it is evidenced |
|---|---|
| Critical thinking & problem solving | Troubleshooting steps in every build; Advanced Questions |
| Communication & collaboration | Team builds; Science Day demos; poster work |
| Creativity & imagination | Design challenges (bridge, machine, capstone) |
| Digital literacy | Optional phone timers, tuner apps, slow-motion video analysis |
| Self-efficacy | Growth marks; portfolio (Unit 16) |
| Learning to learn | Pre/post reflection habit; portfolio self-assessment |

## Safety in Assessment

Never grade speed of a build where that would encourage corner-cutting on safety
(windmill blades, sling range, egg-drop heights). Grade the *method and the data*.

## Reporting to Parents

One page per term: growth trend, project rubric marks, and a photo of the learner's
best build with their own caption. That is a CBE story a parent can read in two minutes.
