# Lesson 124: Unit Review: Energy Expo

**Unit:** 5 — Work, Energy & Power | **Duration:** 45 minutes | **Ages:** 10–17 (differentiated)
**CBE Alignment:** G10-PHY-1.6;JS-FE | **Hands-On:** No | **Project:** —

## Learning Outcomes
By the end of the lesson, learners can:
1. Review and consolidate key concepts from Unit 5.
2. Solve mixed problems involving work, energy, and power.
3. Self-assess understanding and identify areas for revision.

## Materials
- Unit 5 review questions
- Calculator
- Notebook for each learner

## Vocabulary
- **Expo** *(maonyesho)* — a display or exhibition of work.
- **Review** *(kujirudia)* — going over material to reinforce learning.
- **Self-assessment** *(kujipima)* — evaluating your own understanding.
- **Mastery** *(uwezo)* — thorough understanding of a topic.

## Pre-Test
1. What is the formula for work?
2. What is the formula for kinetic energy?
3. What is the formula for power?

**Answer Key (Pre-Test):**
1. W = F × d.
2. KE = ½mv².
3. P = W/t or P = F × v.

## Lesson Flow (45 Minutes)

1. **(5 min)** Settle and administer the Pre-Test. Collect by show of hands.

2. **(5 min)** Engage: Announce "Energy Expo" — groups set up stations reviewing different topics.

3. **(15 min)** Explore:
   - **Activity:** Groups rotate through stations.
   - **Station 1: Work.** Calculate work in lifting, pushing, carrying scenarios.
   - **Station 2: Energy forms.** Identify PE, KE, chemical, heat in photos.
   - **Station 3: Transformations.** Trace energy chains (jiko, body, machine).
   - **Station 4: Conservation.** Solve problems where total energy is conserved.
   - **Station 5: Power.** Calculate and compare human vs machine power.
   - **Station 6: Efficiency.** Calculate efficiency and suggest improvements.
   - Groups spend 2–3 minutes at each station.

4. **(8 min)** Explain:
   - **Key formulas review:**
     - W = Fd = mgh (for lifting).
     - KE = ½mv².
     - PE = mgh.
     - P = W/t.
     - Efficiency = (useful out / total in) × 100%.
   - **Common errors:**
     - Confusing work and power.
     - Using wrong units (hours vs seconds).
     - Forgetting that PE depends on height, not path.
   - **Self-assessment:** Rate confidence 1–5 for each topic.

5. **(10 min)** Post-Test (unit quiz, see below). Learners work individually.

6. **(2 min)** Wrap: Groups share one thing they learned and one thing they want to revise. Homework: complete self-assessment.

## Post-Test
1. *(Mirrors Pre-Test Q1)* What is the formula for work?
2. *(Mirrors Pre-Test Q2)* What is the formula for kinetic energy?
3. A 60 kg pupil climbs 3 m of stairs in 6 s. Calculate power output.
4. A motor is 25% efficient. If it produces 500 J of useful work, how much energy was input?
5. Explain why a bouncing ball stops, using conservation of energy.

**Answer Key (Post-Test):**
1. **W = F × d**.
2. **KE = ½mv²**.
3. W = 60 × 10 × 3 = 1800 J. P = 1800/6 = **300 W**.
4. Input = 500 / 0.25 = **2000 J**.
5. With each bounce, some energy becomes **heat and sound**. Total energy conserved, but mechanical energy decreases.

## Advanced Questions (Ages 14–17)

**Q:** A hydroelectric dam stores water at 100 m height. The reservoir contains 10 million kg of water. Calculate (a) the gravitational potential energy stored, (b) the power output if this water flows out in 2 hours, assuming 90% efficiency, and (c) how many 100 W bulbs this could power.

**Worked Answer:**
(a) PE = mgh = 10⁷ × 10 × 100 = **10¹⁰ J** (10 GJ).

(b) Time = 2 hours = 7200 s.
    Useful energy = 10¹⁰ × 0.90 = 9 × 10⁹ J.
    Power = 9 × 10⁹ / 7200 = **1.25 × 10⁶ W** = **1.25 MW**.

(c) Bulbs = 1,250,000 / 100 = **12,500 bulbs**.

This is a small hydro plant. Large dams like Seven Forks produce 50–100 MW.

**Q:** Write a higher-order question about work, energy, or power that requires analysis rather than recall. Provide a marking scheme.

**Worked Answer:**
**Question:** A family switches from an open fire (efficiency 10%) to an improved jiko (efficiency 35%). They previously used 4 bags of charcoal per month at 800 KSh each. Calculate (a) their new monthly charcoal use, (b) annual savings, and (c) the payback period if the jiko costs 1500 KSh. Discuss one non-financial benefit.

**Marking scheme:**
(a) New charcoal: same useful heat, less waste.
    Old useful heat: 4 bags × 0.10 = 0.4 bags equivalent.
    New bags: 0.4 / 0.35 = **1.14 bags** (3 marks).

(b) Annual savings: old cost = 4 × 800 = 3200/month.
    New cost = 1.14 × 800 = 912/month.
    Savings = 2288/month. Annual = **27,456 KSh** (3 marks).

(c) Payback: 1500 / 2288 = **0.66 months** (2 marks).

Non-financial: reduced indoor air pollution, less deforestation, faster cooking, etc. (2 marks).
Total: **10 marks**.
